Overview

Kerala in this demo centres on backwater calm, coastal air, and unhurried sightseeing. Travellers often combine a houseboat or canal stretch with easy coastal or hillside time.

Best time
Illustrative: October–March for comfortable sightseeing; monsoon months bring lush scenery with heavier rain.
Recommended duration
4–6 days for a backwater-focused retreat

Why Visit Kerala?

  • Quiet backwater and canal scenery
  • Relaxed pace suited to couples and families
  • Coastal towns and green inland drives
  • Houseboat-style overnight or day cruises (illustrative)

Places to Visit in Kerala

  • Alleppey backwaters

  • Canal village stretches

  • Coastal promenades

  • Tea-country viewpoints (optional add-on)
