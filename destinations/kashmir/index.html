Overview

Kashmir is an illustrative demo destination framed around Dal Lake, meadow drives, and cool mountain air. Travellers typically balance Srinagar’s lakeside pace with day trips to Gulmarg, Pahalgam, or Sonamarg — choosing seasons for gardens and mild weather, or for snow-focused scenery.

Illustrative destination profile for Kashmir — lakeside stays, mountain views, and slow travel moments for families and couples.

Best time
Illustrative: March–June for mild valley weather and gardens; July–August for lush greens (expect rain spells); December–February for snow-focused scenery — exact timing depends on travel style.
Recommended duration
5–7 days for a balanced Srinagar + Gulmarg + Pahalgam style circuit

Why Visit Kashmir?

  • Lakeside stays and shikara moments on Dal Lake
  • Easy family-paced sightseeing with mountain backdrops
  • Seasonal contrast — spring gardens through winter snow scenes
  • Comfortable circuit options linking Srinagar, Gulmarg, and Pahalgam
  • Calm evenings suited to couples and multi-generational groups

Places to Visit in Kashmir

Signature stops on an illustrative valley circuit — not an exhaustive inventory.

  • Dal Lake & Boulevard

  • Mughal Gardens

  • Gulmarg

  • Pahalgam

    River valleys, short walks, and quieter stays

  • Sonamarg

    Illustrative high-meadow day trip when road conditions allow
