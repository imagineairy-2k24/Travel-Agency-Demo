Overview

Kashmir is framed around Dal Lake, meadow drives, and cool mountain air. Travellers typically balance Srinagar’s lakeside pace with day trips to Gulmarg, Pahalgam, or Sonamarg — choosing seasons for gardens and mild weather, or for snow-focused scenery.

Best time
March–June for mild valley weather; July–August for lush greens; December–February for snow scenes — timing depends on travel style.
Recommended duration
5–7 days for a balanced Srinagar + Gulmarg + Pahalgam style circuit

Why Visit Kashmir?

  • Lakeside stays and shikara moments on Dal Lake
  • Easy family-paced sightseeing with mountain backdrops
  • Seasonal contrast — spring gardens through winter snow
  • Circuits linking Srinagar, Gulmarg, and Pahalgam
  • Calm evenings for couples and multi-generational groups
